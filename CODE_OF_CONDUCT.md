# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be respectful, be patient, be constructive. This community exists to make developer tools better, and that work is hard enough without hostility.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Intimidation, trolling, or sustained disruption
- Discriminatory language or jokes
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Spam or off-topic promotion

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other venue a maintainer moderates for the project.

## Reporting

To report a violation, contact the maintainer listed in `MAINTAINERS.md` privately through any channel listed there. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will respond to reports promptly and take action proportionate to the violation: a warning, a temporary block, or a permanent ban. Decisions are made by the maintainer and recorded privately; appeals can be made by replying to the enforcement notice.
