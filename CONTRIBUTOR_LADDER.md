# Contributor Ladder

| Rung | Requirements | Privileges | Promotion |
|---|---|---|---|
| Contributor | one merged contribution | credit in release notes | automatic |
| Reviewer | several high-quality reviews, per the community response SLOs | review requests; PR approval counts toward merge | maintainer invitation |
| Maintainer | sustained reviews and contributions over months; trust to merge and release | merge, release, and moderation rights | maintainer decision |

Promotion is by invitation after the requirements are met; there is no voting. Removal happens only after repeated, documented problems: inactivity does not remove anyone, but an inactive maintainer's areas get re-assigned so nothing blocks on them.

This ladder is the operational detail of `GOVERNANCE.md`, which is authoritative on decision-making.
