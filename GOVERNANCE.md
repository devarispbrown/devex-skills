# Governance

This project is founder-led. This document describes how it actually operates today, and how that changes as contributors step up.

## How decisions are made

- The maintainer (listed in `MAINTAINERS.md`) makes final decisions on scope, design, and releases.
- Significant decisions happen in public: pull requests and their reviews are the decision records. Design rationale goes in the PR description, not in a private chat.
- Community input is actively sought on issues and discussions; disagreement with the maintainer is fine, decision records should show why the chosen path won.

## The contributor ladder

The ladder lives in `CONTRIBUTOR_LADDER.md`: Contributor, Reviewer, Maintainer, with requirements, privileges, and promotion per rung. Promotion is by invitation after the requirements are met; there is no voting.

## Scope

This governance covers this repository and its community spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions). It is intentionally small; revisit it when the project grows past a handful of regular contributors.
