# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Our standards

Be respectful and constructive. Harassment, discrimination, and other exclusionary behavior are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report violations by emailing conduct@example.com or by opening a private issue in the repository's meta repository. All reports are handled confidentially by the maintainers.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review reported incidents and take action, up to and including a temporary or permanent ban. Consequences are communicated to the reporter and the person involved.
