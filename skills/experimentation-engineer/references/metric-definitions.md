# Experiment Metric Definitions

Canonical definitions for DX experiment metrics. Reference these definitions by name in every experiment report; do not restate them differently.

Suite constants in `dx-standards/metrics.md` are referenced by name and never restated with different values.

## Task completion

Share of participants who reach and independently verify the intended outcome.

- numerator: sessions that complete and verify
- denominator: sessions assigned to the variant
- direction: up

Completion is not reached on install, auth, or a health check alone; the outcome must be the product's core value.

## Time

Wall-clock time from the start condition to the verified outcome.

- start: the developer opens the flow (quickstart, command, error)
- stop: the developer verifies a meaningful result
- includes installation, authentication, configuration, execution, waiting, and verification
- direction: down

The magic-path timer definition applies to onboarding experiments (`MAGIC_PATH_MAX_MIN` = 15).

## Error rate

Share of sessions with at least one error the developer must handle.

- count any unresolved error or failed step per session
- report per variant, never averaged across variants
- direction: down

Recovery time is separate; see Time and Support load. Suite `TTR_TARGET_MIN` = 5 targets recovery from a known error to corrective action.

## Drop-off

Share of sessions that abandon before the outcome, measured at a defined point.

- measure per step, command, or screen
- report the step with the largest drop-off per variant
- direction: down

Drop-off explains where completion is lost; use it to form the next hypothesis.

## Support load

Support effort generated by the variant per 100 sessions.

- tickets, escalations, or help-community posts attributable to the flow
- recovery time counts when measurable
- direction: down

A treatment that improves completion but raises support load is not a free win.

## Retention

Repeat use of the flow or product over the measurement window.

- return sessions per participant within the window
- window is set before launch and stated in the report
- direction: up

Retention windows differ per surface: quickstarts measure next-day return, error recovery measures corrected retry.

## Measurement rules

- define every metric and its window before launch
- label every value Observed, CI-observed, or Estimated
- unlabeled values are UNVERIFIED and cannot drive a ship decision
- compare each treatment to control; never average variants
- report guardrail metrics even when they do not move
